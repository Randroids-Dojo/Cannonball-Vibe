## What changed (`interior.py`)

- `LOD0_Binnacle`: rounded box (0.40 by 0.22 by 0.13 m, 50 mm bevel) with
  the upper half drawn 30 mm back and narrowed 10 percent, tilted 8
  degrees, in the dash leather.
- `LOD0_ClusterBezel` and `LOD0_CentreScreenBezel` behind the screens,
  in the dark plastic; the cluster tilts 10 degrees to the driver.
- `LOD0_Vent_0..3` with two vanes each at x = -0.64, -0.13, 0.13, 0.64 m,
  30 mm under the dash top.
- `LOD0_StartButton` on the console; the steering hub is 25 percent wider
  than tall.
