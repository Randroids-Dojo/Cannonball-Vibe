# P1-008 Hero GT generation 3, lamps slice: the car lights the road

- Date: 2026-09-04
- Task: P1-008 (Q-020 Option A, project-original grand tourer), following
  [the paint and cockpit audit](2026-09-04-p1-008-hero-gt-paint-and-cockpit.md)
- Evidence: `evidence/M5/P1-008-hero-gt-gen3-lamps.json`
- Review sheet: `docs/images/p1-008-hero-gt-gen3-lamps-review.png`

## Outcome

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Did the car light the road before this? | No. `Light_Head_FL`, `Light_Head_FR`, `Light_Tail_RL` and `Light_Tail_RR` have been contract anchors since P1-002, resolved and validated, with nothing under them. The run starts at night by design (`Main.BuildLighting`), so every night frame showed an unlit car on a moonlit road. |
| What is under the anchors now? | Per headlamp a warm low beam (`SpotLight3D`, 30 energy, 120 m range, 38 degrees, dipped 2.2 degrees, shadowed) and a 3 m spill; per tail lamp a small red glow. They are children of the anchors, so they follow steering-independent body motion and every suspension state the rig applies. |
| Who switches them? | `SkyLighting` now publishes `CurrentPreset` and raises `PresetChanged` when `Apply` changes it; the rig lights the lamps for Night and Dawn and douses them for Day and Overcast, starting from whatever preset is current when it enters the tree (night, if none has been applied). No scenario or the main game calls anything new. |
| Does review cover it? | The night cockpit stage asserts the lamps are lit and the daylight chase stage asserts they are not; the walk-around runs its second half at night, so the sheet shows the beams and tail glow from outside. |

## What changed

- `SkyLighting.cs`: `CurrentPreset`, `PresetChanged`, `LampsLit(preset)`.
- `VehicleVisualRig.cs`: `BuildLamps` under the four light anchors,
  `SetHeadlights`, `HeadlightsOn`, subscription in `_Ready` released in
  `_ExitTree`; `headlights_on`, `lamp_count` and `beam_shadows` in the
  automation state. `ApplyLampEmission` scales the tail bar and projector
  lens emission to 12 percent of the source strength when the lamps are
  doused, remembering the source per material name, so the tail does not
  glow by day; the LED strips stay as running lights. Beam shadows are off
  when `--graybox-environment-assets` is set: the semantic suite runs the
  production rig on software renderers behind that flag, two shadowed beams
  cost the macOS camera test its rear-view settle window (0.85 of the 0.9
  bound, run 33907041405), and nothing in that suite looks at a beam shadow.
- `CannonballVehicle.cs`: `SetHeadlights` passthrough.
- `VehicleVisualScenario.cs`: stage assertions above; walk-around night half.

## Claims not made

- The reference capture (`capture-reference-performance.sh`, Balanced,
  2560 by 1440, chase camera, 60 s measured on the RTX 3080 Ti) puts the
  night run with the lamps at 3.43 ms mean frame time (GPU 2.90 ms, p99
  5.10 ms, 1,326 draw calls) against 2.95 ms by day without them (GPU
  2.43 ms, p99 4.20 ms, 1,158 draw calls). The difference bounds the cost of
  the two shadowed beams and the night preset together at under half a
  millisecond on the reference PC; it is not a claim for the Low tier, where
  the beams can drop their shadow in one place if Q-041 wants it. The
  capture ran with other work on the machine (`--allow-contended`).
- No high beam, no indicator lamps, no interior lighting at night.
- The LED strips glow by day as running lights by design.
