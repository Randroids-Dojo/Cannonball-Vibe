# Meridian S8R packaging revision 7

2026-09-11. Independent source16 QA evaluated 645 native poses, including one-percent increments near each closed position. These samples exposed small clashes that the previous coarse opening checks missed. The evidence remains under `reports/p1-018/qa/production-16/`; zero sampled intersections alone will not be reported as proof of continuous clearance.

The original hood hinge rises from Z=0.972 m to 0.990 m to clear the rear cowl during initial opening. Its moving arms and bearing bores follow that axis. Hood underside ribs move outside the airbox/strut envelope and attach to the actual underside. The trunk trailing edge moves from Y=-2.432 m to -2.385 m, ahead of the fixed optical housings; the cutout and inner hem follow it. Both closure perimeter heights are evaluated against the original pre-cut body surface, including its front bow. Hood crown falls to zero at the perimeter. Front fender inset edges provide a 3.5 mm nominal hood side gap.

Window belts and rear glazing terminate inside their actual moving door boundaries. Front upper glazing clears the A-pillar. Rear speakers move aft of the B-pillar seal, cabin grab handles move inboard, and the front upper aperture seal follows the door return. These are original packaging corrections rather than newly inferred benchmark measurements.

The auxiliary vent pickup moves clear of the tank strap, the transfer pump moves 10 mm inboard of the rear wheel tub, and the quarter-panel vent union uses a narrow shank within the drilled passage plus an interior flange. The fuel-line floor gland is explicitly compressible rubber: its 19 mm outer radius seats in an 18 mm bore, allowing a bounded 1 mm nominal radial interference. Rigid metal intersections are not covered by that contact policy. Mating pipe/union seats must be inspected and classified individually.

The isolated front-bumper paint bevel threshold changes from 0.45 to 1.0 rad. Matched native renders in `reports/p1-018/research/surface-shading-15/` identify two smooth profile joins accidentally caught by the lower threshold; the higher threshold removes the triangular highlight without changing the intended silhouette or increasing tessellation. Broader surface quality remains subject to final visual review.

Revision6 is preserved. Factory dimensions, capacity accounting, gameplay speed limits and human approval boundaries are unchanged. This revision requires new source, export, posed-clearance, visual and runtime evidence before acceptance.
