# Meridian S8R packaging revision 4

2026-09-11. This is a correction to original fictional closure construction in P1-018, after independent actual-mesh and rendered inspection of production checkpoint 10. Revision 3 is preserved in `specification-v3.json`.

The rear door axes move from Y=-0.580 m to -0.625 m. The previous axis lay forward of the closed front door's trailing return and placed the rear upper hinge leaf inside that door. The new axis sits behind the front closure and immediately ahead of the rear door edge. X=+/-0.915 m and Z=0.500 m, door opening angles, wheelbase, occupant positions and gameplay setup are unchanged. This is a candidate requiring renewed full-assembly clearance testing, not a factory-derived hinge specification.

The body aperture cuts extend through the shoulder so no fixed belt-height shell bridges an open doorway. Rear inner trim gains a curved wheel-well relief and a shorter storage pocket. Hood and trunk hinge assemblies use distinct central moving and outer fixed knuckles around a separate pin, with a 0.5 mm radial pin-to-bore clearance. Broad fixed/moving plate penetration is not an accepted interface. Fuel pipes receive actual aligned floor passages and a sealed chase. All changes require topology, posed geometry and actual rendered reinspection.

The tire circumference uses 64 segments: maximum ideal-circle chord deviation at radius 0.3433 m is 0.414 mm, below the 0.5 mm radius modeling tolerance. This is geometric approximation error and does not change the locked rolling radius or source uncertainty.

No rights, visual-quality, usability or driving-feel human gate is closed by this revision.
