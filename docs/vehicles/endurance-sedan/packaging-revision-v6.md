# Meridian S8R packaging revision 6

2026-09-11. Source11 actual posed meshes showed the outer leading rear-door return rotating into the fixed B-pillar strip. The rear hinge axis was inboard of that return. Revision6 moves the two original rear hinge axes to X=+/-0.942 m, with Y=-0.625 m and Z=0.500 m retained. The 8 mm pin radius stays within the 0.950 m half-body envelope. This deliberately uses compact outboard hinge hardware; it is original construction, not a claim about the benchmark Audi's hidden hinge layout. Renewed full-assembly pose and appearance checks are required.

The front upper door boundary and mirror-stalk root now leave the diagonal A-pillar return clear. Rear inner hems follow the actual closure boundary instead of extending into the fixed wheel-quarter structure. The headliner terminates inside the roof-side rail rather than crossing the moving door frame. Hood/trunk offset arms now have a real bore coaxial with their pins, in addition to the separate knuckle bores. The transfer-line body passage follows the actual pipe centerline.

The instrument anchor rises another10 mm to Z=1.060 m. An actual fixed-eye source13 render made every required numeric value readable, but independent QA found a pixel-scale rim margin beneath the fuel digits. This change adds margin without changing the eye, seat, column or input contract. Source and native runtime views must verify it with warnings, signals and steering states.

All revisions are original packaging choices. Prior v5 is preserved, factory research and gameplay policies remain separate, and human approvals remain open.
