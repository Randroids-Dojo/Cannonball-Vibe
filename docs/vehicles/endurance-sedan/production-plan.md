Claim date:2026-09-10. Scope is defined in DELIVERY_LEDGER.json.

## Ownership

The lead is the sole writer of `endurance-sedan.blend`, its construction/export
scripts, geometry, materials, semantic contract and generated outputs. Research
owns `research.md` and `reference-values.json`. Runtime owns agreed vehicle
adapter/wrapper and new scenario source paths, after the lead locks the contract.
QA owns `defects.json` and independent observations/captures under
`reports/p1-018/qa/`. Shared files are coordinated through the lead.

## Ordered stages

1. Compare Audi S6 C7 facelift, AMG E63 S and BMW M5; resolve market/year/trim,
   units and confidence. Lock engineering dimensions, original design decisions,
   modeling tolerances, interactions and provisional costs before detail.
2. Build fresh meter-scale blockout with wheel/contact, suspension, collision,
   camera and occupant anchors. Prove the existing physics and integration.
3. Refine silhouette, panels and assemblies; inspect orthographic, clay and
   reflection views before microdetail. Build complete cabin and visible systems.
4. Add state-driven presentation and inspection controls in owned wrappers;
   test full opening, steering, suspension and wiper motion.
5. Produce LODs, validate and export twice, reopen source and clean import;
   measure actual runtime performance and capture all specified review media.
6. Independent visual/functional QA, defect corrections, full required gates,
   evidence, auto-squash PR integration and explicit human-review handoff.

The primary benchmark and fictional identity are not selected in this claim.
No new performance target, rights approval, handling approval or completed art is
claimed. Geometry is new; the existing Hero GT remains available.
