# Meridian S8R packaging revision 3 — 2026-09-10

Revision 2 is preserved in `specification-v2.json`. Source07 evaluated geometry
and the independent bounded hinge-axis search demonstrated that its inboard
door axes pushed the outer leading edge into the adjacent fixed body during
initial rotation. The parked mesh alone could not establish this fit.

Revision 3 locks conventional vertical front-door axes at X ±0.915 m,
Y +0.720 m, Z 0.500 m, and rear-door axes at X ±0.915 m, Y −0.580 m,
Z 0.500 m. The front shell candidate cleared the search's 104 actual mesh poses;
that result does not establish clearance for its complete assembly. The rear
shell still requires local inner-jamb relief. Complete post-build inspection,
including stationary neighboring doors, remains mandatory.

The hood and trunk pivot heights become 0.972 m and 0.990 m respectively.
Their X/Y locations and open angles remain as in revision 2. Raising these
axes moves the opening rear/leading skin edges above the fixed cowl/deck and
parked wiper assemblies. The mounting points receive real recessed pockets;
moving hinges cannot be excused as passing through uncut solid bodywork.

The body-side B pillar moves inboard of the inner door envelopes. Fixed A/C
pillar skins are relieved away from the moving window frames. Aperture seals
follow the actual wheel-arch boundary and sit behind the inner door cards.
These are original construction choices, subject to the same geometry,
panel-gap and motion-clearance tolerances as the initial specification.

All researched factory values, outside dimensions, tires, wheelbase/tracks,
physics and camera setup remain unchanged. The prior fuel/liner, exhaust/rail
and bulkhead-passage corrections are retained. No approval or simulated
mechanical-performance claim is introduced by this revision.
