# Meridian S8R retained evidence

These archives preserve actual construction and verification history for
P1-018. Archive verification establishes retained bytes; individual reports
retain their original failures, limited scope and approval status.

`root-history-through31.zip` contains 602 original checkpoint, command, input
snapshot, measured inventory and actual render/movie files. Its two separately
written copies have the same SHA-256:
`c1d2436014f8eb948758d182f23d082290a268c0561946988dc23bd3d5d98da4`.
The archive has 396,615,257 bytes. [The retention index](root-history-through31.json)
lists every member, its SHA-256, the command/tool context and the retained scope.

`research-history-v3.zip` preserves 1,306 research, specification, provenance,
diagnostic and correction records in 41,658,838 bytes. Both separately written
copies have SHA-256
`0a128b6d7668c658fea1244e99f6966ff2214fc7d2e7a292950be186a808fc6f`.
[Its retention index](research-history-v3.json) includes the exact member inventory
and the exclusions for 64 external reference downloads. Those excluded files
retain source URLs or distribution identifiers and checksums; they are not
redistributed as project-authored content.

[Research supplement v1](research-supplement-v1.json) binds553 later material,
runtime-contract, revision15 and CI checkout records. Its59,210,935-byte archive
was independently written twice with SHA-256
`df5b7811fa0dadfdce5f4c7426504a0704c5ecece2e40f6452161152bbf54ae1`.
The actual material comparison frames and failed attempts are retained;
15 downloaded external references remain excluded with their source metadata.
Revision14 document snapshots remain alongside revision15 so historical
manifests are reproducible from their own extracted input snapshots.

Extract to a new directory. `manifest.json` lists the preserved repository-relative
paths, including retained scripts where explicitly inventoried. Open a historical `.blend`
with Blender 5.1.2 when reproducing a defect. Historical sources and exports are
diagnostic checkpoints, including rejected candidates. The shipping source and
final acceptance evidence are separate artifacts.

The first inventory accidentally included its own unfinished stdout log.
Readback detected the changed hash before packaging. The corrected inventory
includes only completed command/log pairs with matching recorded hashes. Both
attempts and the correction remain in the archive. Every member's checksum and
the archive CRC were verified after writing both copies.

Manufacturer reference downloads, external photographs, application binaries,
credentials and copied runtime project caches are excluded. Bfont-derived label
geometry in the original model checkpoints retains its documented output-rights
review requirement. Human visual, rights, handling and usability approval is not
implied by retaining these files.

The six `runtime-history01-part-*.zip` archives retain the first frozen runtime
history inventory. Each was written twice, read back with CRC and every member
SHA-256 verification, and compared byte for byte. The complete inventory also
records953 exact aliases and four explicitly omitted intermediate movies;
their canonical payload paths or omission reasons remain in the inventory.
